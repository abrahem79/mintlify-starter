## How It Works

Getting started with Google takes just a few minutes. Follow these three steps to go from zero to fully integrated.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Google Account">
    Visit [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com) and sign up for a free Google Account. Your account is your passport to every Google service — Search, Gmail, Drive, Cloud, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the Services You Need">
    Head to the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com) to create a project and enable the specific APIs and services your use case requires, such as the Custom Search API, Maps API, or Gmail API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Apps via the API">
    Generate API credentials in the Cloud Console and use Google's REST or client-library APIs to integrate Google services directly into your application. Check out the [Quickstart](/quickstart) to make your first API call in minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

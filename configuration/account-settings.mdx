Your Google Account dashboard is the central hub for managing everything about your identity, privacy, and storage across all Google services. Whether you want to update a profile photo, change your display language, or audit how much space your emails are consuming, every setting is accessible from a single location at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).

## Personal Info

Your personal information controls how you appear across Google products — from your name in Gmail signatures to your profile picture in Google Meet. You can update these details at any time from the **Personal info** section of your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Google Account">
    Navigate to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com) and sign in if prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Personal info">
    In the left sidebar, click **Personal info**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a field">
    Click on any field — such as your name, photo, birthday, or gender — to edit it. Click **Save** when you're done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The table below describes each personal info field and what it affects:

| Field | Description | Visibility |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Your full name shown across Google products | Can be public or limited |
| **Profile photo** | Image shown in Gmail, Meet, and Workspace | Can be public or limited |
| **Birthday** | Used to personalize your experience and verify age | Private by default |
| **Gender** | Used to personalize pronouns in Google products | You control who can see this |

<Note>
  Some fields like birthday and gender let you independently control who can see them. Click the visibility icon next to any field to adjust who has access.
</Note>

## Language & Region

Google uses your language and region settings to determine the interface language, search results locale, date formats, and regional content across all services.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Google Account">
    Go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Data & privacy or General preferences">
    In the left sidebar, click **Data & privacy**, then scroll to **General preferences for the web**, or go directly to the **Language** section within your account settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your display language">
    Click the language listed under **Preferred language**, then select your preferred language from the list and click **Select**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your region">
    Scroll to **Region & language** and update your country or region setting to match your current location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | Where to Change | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Display language** | Account → Language | Changes the Google UI language |
| **Regional format** | Account → Language → Region | Adjusts date, time, and number formats |
| **Search region** | google.com → Settings → Search settings | Controls the country context for search results |

<Tip>
  Changing your account language does not automatically change the language Google Search uses. You can set your Search language separately at [google.com/preferences](https://www.google.com/preferences).
</Tip>

## Privacy Controls

Google provides several privacy controls that let you decide how your data is collected and used to personalize your experience. You can access all of these from the **Data & privacy** section of your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data & privacy">
    At [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com), click **Data & privacy** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Activity controls">
    Under **History settings**, click **Web & App Activity**, **Location History**, or **YouTube History** to review and pause any of these individually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust Ad personalization">
    Scroll to **Ad settings** and click **Ad personalization** to toggle whether Google uses your activity to show personalized ads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review what data is saved">
    Click **My Activity** to see a full timeline of your recorded activity across Google services. You can delete individual entries or set up auto-delete schedules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Privacy Control | What It Does | Default State |
|---|---|---|
| **Web & App Activity** | Saves your searches and activity for personalized results | On |
| **Location History** | Records places you've been via your devices | Off (varies by device) |
| **YouTube History** | Saves videos you watch to improve recommendations | On |
| **Ad Personalization** | Uses your data to show more relevant ads | On |

<Warning>
  Pausing an activity control stops future data from being saved, but does not delete data already stored. To remove past activity, use the **Delete activity by** option within My Activity.
</Warning>

## Account Storage

Your Google Account comes with 15 GB of free storage shared across Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Photos. Once you reach your limit, you won't be able to send or receive emails, upload files, or back up photos until you free up space or upgrade.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your current storage usage">
    Go to [one.google.com/storage](https://one.google.com/storage) to see a breakdown of storage used by Gmail, Drive, and Photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Free up space">
    Click **Free up account storage** to get personalized suggestions — such as deleting large email attachments, emptying the trash in Drive, or removing blurry photos in Google Photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade your storage (optional)">
    Click **Get more storage** to upgrade to a Google One plan, which starts at 100 GB and includes additional member benefits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Service | What Counts Toward Quota | What Doesn't Count |
|---|---|---|
| **Gmail** | Emails and attachments | Emails from Google (promotions managed by Google) |
| **Google Drive** | Files, PDFs, images, videos | Google Docs, Sheets, Slides (native formats) |
| **Google Photos** | Photos and videos backed up | (All photos count as of June 2021) |

<Note>
  Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, and Drawings created natively in Google Workspace do **not** count against your storage quota, but files converted from other formats (such as .docx or .xlsx) do.
</Note>

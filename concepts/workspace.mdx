Google Workspace is an integrated suite of cloud-based productivity applications that lets your team create, communicate, and collaborate from anywhere — no software installation required. Because every app is built on the same platform, files live in Drive, identities are managed through a single Google account, and real-time co-editing works consistently across Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Whether you're a solo creator or part of a large enterprise, Workspace scales with you through a range of plans that add administrative controls, compliance features, and pooled storage as your needs grow.

## Apps at a Glance

Workspace is made up of several tightly integrated applications. Here's a quick reference to the most commonly used ones.

## Sharing and Permissions

Workspace uses a role-based permission model for files and folders stored in Drive. When you share an item, you assign one of four roles that determine what the recipient can do.

| Role | Can view | Can comment | Can edit | Can manage sharing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Viewer** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Commenter** | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Editor** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Owner** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

A few important rules to keep in mind:

- Every file has exactly one **Owner**. Ownership can be transferred to another user in the same Workspace organisation, but not to a personal Google account.
- **Editors** can re-share a file with others by default. If you want to restrict this, open the sharing settings and disable **Editors can change permissions and share**.
- You can share with specific individuals, with anyone who has the link, or (on Business and Enterprise plans) restrict sharing to members of your organisation only.

<Warning>
  Setting a file to "Anyone with the link can edit" makes it publicly editable by anyone who obtains the URL — even people outside your organisation. Reserve this setting for content that is intentionally public and low-risk.
</Warning>

## Storage

Workspace storage is **pooled across your organisation** rather than allocated per user. Your organisation's total storage capacity is shared among all users and all content in Drive, Gmail, and Photos.

Key things to know about storage:

- **What counts toward storage:** Files you own in Drive (including Docs, Sheets, and Slides), email messages and attachments in Gmail, and photos and videos in Google Photos.
- **What doesn't count:** Items in your Trash (until permanently deleted), items shared with you that you don't own, and Google-native files (Docs, Sheets, Slides) created before June 2021.
- **Quotas:** If your organisation reaches its storage limit, users won't be able to upload new files, send or receive email, or back up photos until storage is freed up or your plan is upgraded.
- **Managing usage:** Admins can monitor usage per user in the Google Admin console and set individual storage limits on Business Starter and Business Standard plans.

<Note>
  On the free tier (personal Google accounts), each account receives 15 GB of storage shared across Drive, Gmail, and Photos. Upgrading to a paid Google One or Workspace plan increases this limit.
</Note>

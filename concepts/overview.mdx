Google's platform brings together a broad set of cloud-based services that cover the full lifecycle of how you find, create, and share information. Whether you're searching the web for answers, collaborating with teammates on a shared document, or building an application that taps into Google's infrastructure, every service is designed to work together seamlessly — giving individuals, teams, and developers a consistent foundation to build on.

## The Three Pillars of Google's Platform

Google organises its core offering around three interconnected areas, each serving a distinct but complementary purpose.

### 1. Search

Google Search is the entry point for billions of queries every day. Beyond web search, Google's search technology powers internal tools, custom search engines embedded in your own products, and APIs that let you retrieve and rank information programmatically. Understanding how Search works helps you surface the right content at the right time — whether for your users or yourself.

### 2. Google Workspace

Google Workspace is Google's suite of cloud-based productivity and collaboration applications. It includes Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Meet, all built around real-time collaboration and accessible from any device. Workspace is designed so that your whole team works in a single shared environment, with granular controls over who can view, comment on, or edit your content.

### 3. Developer APIs

Google exposes a rich set of APIs that give you programmatic access to its services — from embedding search results in your application, to reading and writing Workspace documents, to integrating authentication, maps, and machine-learning models. The APIs follow standard REST and gRPC conventions, use OAuth 2.0 for authorisation, and are documented in full in the API reference section of these docs.
